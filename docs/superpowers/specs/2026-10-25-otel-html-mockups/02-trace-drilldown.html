Layout. The panel slides over the list and keeps a narrow column of it visible. Click another row, or press j / k, to switch traces without closing. Esc closes the panel. "Full page" opens the trace at its own URL, and so does Copy link.

Header. Service › resource › trace ID, then a meta line: duration percentile (among the same service and resource), duration, method and URL, status code, error state, time, and counts.

Trace tabs. Flame graph, Waterfall, Span list and Services (a simple per-service table; no node map in v1). Then trace-wide Logs and Errors.

Flame graph. Filter spans, errors only, color by service/host/scope, a minimap with fit/zoom, and an exec-time legend. Error spans get a red outline, a "!" badge and a hatch pattern, so they can't be confused with a service color. A drag handle sets the split between the graph and the span detail.

Span tabs, OTel only. Overview (exceptions first, then pinned attributes and attributes grouped by semantic-convention namespace), Events, Logs, Metrics, Links (span links) and Resource (host.*, process.*, container.* and scope; covers infrastructure and process context). Network and Profiles are left out until OTel data can fill them.

Reuse rule. Embedded tabs are the explorer components with filters already applied. The Logs tab is the Log Explorer's list with trace_id/span_id tokens and a scope switch (span · trace · service ±30s). The Metrics tab is the Metrics Explorer's chart with a "this span" marker band, and exemplars are highlighted when they point to this trace. "Open in … Explorer" carries the same filter tokens across.
