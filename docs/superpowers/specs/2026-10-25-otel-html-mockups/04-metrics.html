Query rows, OTel-aware. Each row has a metric (type badge and unit taken from OTLP), where (the same filter tokens as Logs and Traces), show, by (group by any resource or data-point attribute), ƒ (rate, increase, rollup, top-N) and alias. The show choices depend on the metric type: Sum offers rate, increase or sum; Gauge offers avg, min, max or last; Histogram and exp-histogram offer p50–p99, count, sum or avg, computed from buckets; Summary offers its quantiles. Cumulative and delta temporality are handled for you.

+ Formula combines queries (a / c, a − b). Display can be Lines, Area, Bars, Heatmap or Distribution; Heatmap and Distribution are for histograms. Linear or log scale, and one chart per query or all on one. Every chart uses a single y-axis.

Linking from a chart. Hover shows a crosshair and values. Clicking pins a menu: jump to an exemplar's trace, or to traces or logs in that time window with the same filters, or zoom the time range. Exemplars appear as ringed points.

"Save to Dashboard" becomes "Copy as dashboard widget". A static report can't save changes, so this copies the widget's config to paste into the writer's dashboard config. Views and Copy link still save explorer state in the URL.

Catalog (replaces Summary / Volume). Lists every metric with its type, unit, temporality, instrumentation scope, attribute keys, series count and a sparkline. Clicking a row opens it in the Explorer.

Embedded grid. One chart card component in S, M or L. Charts are grouped by semantic-convention namespace (process.*, http.*, …) and filtered to the resource of the log or span you came from. A "this log" or "this span" marker sits at that moment, exemplars that point to the related trace are highlighted, and "Open in Explorer" carries the filters over.
